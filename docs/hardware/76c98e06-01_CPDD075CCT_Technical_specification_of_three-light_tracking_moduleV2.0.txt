CPDD075CCT Technical specification of three-light tracking module

Product Introduction

CPDD075CCT Three-Light AI Tracking Module is equipped with one long-focus visible light CMOS sensor, one short-focus visible light CMOS sensor, and one long-wave uncooled infrared camera. The short-focus visible light camera can output analog visible light video with extremely low latency; the long-focus visible light camera features autonomous recognition, locking, tracking, and guidance capabilities for targets such as people, vehicles, and windows; and the infrared camera provides the capability to perform tasks in all weather and at all times.

Application Scenarios

FPV drone vision module, anti-drone visual guidance module, AI tracking module.
